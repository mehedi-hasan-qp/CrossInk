## Crash Found: OOM in Font Decompressor

Opening `test_bangla.epub` crashes the `bangla` variant firmware. The crash is an `abort()` triggered by OOM.

### Root cause (two-stage failure)

1. **Prewarm stage** (`FontDecompressor.cpp:415`): `malloc(135264)` fails for group 6 (all 770 PUA conjunct glyphs in one group). Logged, skipped — non-fatal.
2. **Render stage** (`FontDecompressor.cpp:173`): `getBitmap()` hot-group fallback calls `hotGroup.resize(135264)`. With `-fno-exceptions`, resize failure → `std::terminate()` → `abort()`. The guard on line 174 (`if (hotGroup.empty())`) is dead code.

### Why group 6 is 135 KB

`fontconvert.py` groups glyphs by Unicode block. The entire PUA range `(0xE000, 0xF8FF)` is one group — all 770 pre-rendered Bangla conjuncts land in it. No glyph count or size cap exists.

Group sizes at 14px: groups 0–5 are all under 10 KB. Group 6 alone = 135,264 bytes uncompressed.

### Fix 1 — `lib/EpdFont/FontDecompressor.cpp:173`
Before `hotGroup.resize()`, check `esp_get_free_heap_size()`. If heap < group size, log and return `nullptr` instead of crashing. Prevents abort; conjuncts show as replacement chars.

### Fix 2 — `lib/EpdFont/scripts/fontconvert.py:1167–1184`
Add `MAX_GROUP_GLYPHS = 200` cap. Post-process oversized groups by splitting into sub-groups. At 14px: 770 glyphs → 4 groups of ~193 glyphs, each ~35 KB uncompressed (safe on device).
